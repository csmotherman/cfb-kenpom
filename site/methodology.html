CFF MethodologyTransparent by design
\n

What the ratings actually mean

\n

CollegeFootballFocus is built to estimate team strength from real games, not recreate the AP Poll. The core table adjusts for the quality of competition so a strong performance against a strong opponent is treated differently from the same raw result against a weak one.

\n
\n\n
\n

Core ratings

\n
\n
\n

AdjEM / CFF

Core
\n

The site's schedule-adjusted Simple Rating System (SRS) strength score. It is computed week by week using games available through that point in the season. Higher is better.

\n
\n
\n

AdjO

Research stage
\n

A schedule-adjusted offensive yards-per-play edge from the opponent-adjustment research model. The value and its national rank are shown together. Higher is better.

\n
\n
\n

AdjD

Research stage
\n

A schedule-adjusted defensive yards-per-play edge. The model is expressed so higher is better for defense too, making positive values consistently represent outperforming expectation.

\n
\n
\n

SOS

Core
\n

Strength of schedule, based on the average SRS strength of opponents played through the selected week or within the selected Advanced range.

\n
\n
\n

Wk Δ

Context
\n

The change in a team's overall CFF rank from the previous week. It helps surface movement without pretending one week is a completely new model.

\n
\n
\n

SOR

Not locked
\n

Strength of record is intentionally blank. The site will not publish a number until the résumé-based definition is specified and validated.

\n
\n
\n
Important: a strength rating and a résumé ranking answer different questions. CFF is primarily trying to describe how strong a team has played, not reward every win equally or reproduce a human poll.
\n
\n\n
\n

Advanced CFF

\n

Advanced Analytics separates metrics that can truly be recalculated for a selected range from model estimates that only exist as a snapshot through an end week.

\n
    \n
  • Selected-range stats: success rate, passing success, rushing success, pace, time of possession, record and range SOS are recomputed from the weeks you choose.
    • \n
  • End-week model snapshots: CFF, adjusted offense/defense, field-position edge, explosiveness and finishing use the model state as of the selected end week.
    • \n
  • Pass / run split: describes offensive tendency or opponent tendency against a defense across the selected range.
    • \n
  • Special teams: intentionally blank until a real source and validated definition are added.
    • \n
\n
\n\n
\n

Data and validation philosophy

\n

Game and play data are CFBD-derived and materialized through the project's canonical data pipeline. The site rebuilds its ratings and Advanced data from that pipeline rather than hand-editing numbers in the UI.

\n

Research-stage opponent-adjustment metrics are labeled instead of presented as finished methodology. The source project has shown improvement versus naive baselines in held-out testing, but those metrics remain explicitly separated from the locked core rating until the production definition is final.
