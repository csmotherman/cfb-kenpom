What has to be true before it is paid

\n

The product should earn the subscription. That means the metrics need stable definitions, the site needs to stay fast on a phone, and Advanced needs to answer questions the free ratings cannot. The beta is being used to harden those pieces before checkout is switched on.

\n
Product principle: the free ratings should be good enough to build trust. The subscription should win because the deeper analysis saves time and reveals information, not because the basic site is deliberately crippled.
